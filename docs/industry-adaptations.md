# Adapt the SOC to the industry process

The examples are synthetic scoping contexts, not client engagements, sector certifications or jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions.

| Context | Decisions that change the architecture | Required owner/evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Government/public services | Local mission authority, citizen-data access, federation, sovereignty and disconnected offices | Agency/process owners, permitted disclosure, local dependency and recovery observations |
| BFSI | Customer/transaction boundaries, independent payment authority, supplier concentration and cryptographic dependencies | Business action owners, denied cross-customer actions and scoped recovery/approval evidence |
| Oil & Gas/industrial | Passive OT visibility, remote sites, maintenance rights and safety interfaces | Process/safety owners, approved collection/testing and locally bounded response |
| Healthcare | Sensitive records, clinical availability, device constraints and consequential containment | Clinical/service and privacy owners; authorized evidence access and continuity decisions |
| Retail/logistics | Peak seasons, payment/customer data, store/warehouse edges and availability | Service owners, burst/backlog evidence and explicit remote-site authority |
| Manufacturing | IT/OT boundaries, intellectual assets, supplier access and scheduled operations | Engineering owners, maintenance permissions and safe recovery conditions |
| Telecom/digital platforms | High-rate telemetry, region/tenant boundaries, outage impact and cost | Platform owners, representative query/ingest distributions and preserved evidence meaning |
| MSSP/MDR service | Customer segregation, retained enterprise duties, staffing handover and bounded response | Per-customer grants/keys, disclosure controls and joint incident exercise |

Apply the 24-layer review to the actual service. Establish the entity, jurisdictions, contracts and authoritative source versions with responsible advisers. A pattern or NIST mapping does not establish compliance with every industry's obligations.
