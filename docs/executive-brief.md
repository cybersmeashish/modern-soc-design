# Modern SOC: the decisions leadership should fund

A SOC connects enterprise visibility to accountable investigation and response. The design must match business-service priorities, decision authority, operating capacity and recovery requirements.

## Six executive decisions

| Decision | Leadership output | Evidence to request |
|---|---|---|
| Which services and harms matter most? | Scope, coverage commitment and accountable owners | Service/dependency map, exclusions and missing-source status |
| Who may see evidence or change a resource? | Human, service, tenant and action authority | Allowed/denied access and exact consequential-action approval cases |
| Where must data, keys, operators and recovery remain? | Sovereignty and disclosure boundaries | Declarations separated from provider, contractual and observed evidence |
| What should be internal or outsourced? | Retained duties, supplier interfaces and exit options | Joint escalation, retained evidence and supplier-failure exercise |
| What workload can the platform and people sustain? | Costed options and capacity/coverage assumptions | Representative ingest/query/queue measurements and observed analyst workload |
| What demonstrates readiness and continued operation? | Acceptance gates and review cadence | Failure, restoration, stale-evidence and authority-state observations |

## Proposed implementation sequence

1. Scope a critical service and establish owners, source/visibility gaps and response rights.
2. Select the architecture constraints before selecting products. Resolve hard conflicts in jurisdiction, connectivity, key custody and operator authority.
3. Pilot the source-to-case and independent action path with legitimate and misuse cases.
4. Measure the intended workload and exercise dependency failures and clean restoration.
5. Obtain responsible-owner acceptance, retain unknowns and plan the next bounded expansion.

Timing, funding and acceptance are agreed for the actual estate. The planning models are assumptions-based lower bounds, not implementation promises or measured financial outcomes.

## What this portfolio establishes

The repository publishes original architecture decisions, 24 layer reviews, 20 pattern diagrams, proposed acceptance criteria and locally checked planning/record tools. It does not claim a deployed SOC, customer results, operational detection coverage or regulatory certification.
