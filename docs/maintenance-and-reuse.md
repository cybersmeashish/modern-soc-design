# Maintain the design as evidence grows

`catalog/layers.json` and `catalog/patterns.json` are the authored inputs. `tools/build_docs.py` rebuilds the chapters, indexes, constraint register and primary-source guide. Make all edits and generation inside the named OrbStack workspace.

Keep stable IDs, scope and provenance when adding material. Add an environment observation as a dated artifact with a defined method rather than replacing a planned acceptance criterion with an unsupported success statement. Link failures and incomplete observations to their unresolved decisions.

The examples are synthetic. Reuse the structure with the real service's accepted owners and constraints. Independent standards, providers and upstream tools retain their own authorship and terms; the repository's original design does not imply their endorsement.
